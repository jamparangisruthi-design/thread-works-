01

Share Your Inspiration

02

Discuss Your Design

Our master designers help you finalize neckline depths, embroidery density, stone colors, and work placement.

03

Crafting Begins

Master artisans meticulously hand-embroider every single stitch, stone, pearl, and zari metallic wire.

04

Finished to perfection, steamed, quality-verified, and dispatched securely directly to your doorstep.
