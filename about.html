The Atelier Story

Where Every Thread Tells a Story

At Sruthi Threads & Designs, we believe that an Indian blouse is not simply a piece of stitched fabric; it is a canvas of heritage, celebration, and emotion. Every knot, bead, and metallic gold wire reflects centuries of Indian royal textile history tailored for the modern woman.

Founded with an unwavering passion for handloom textiles and bridal embellishments, our Hyderabad-based atelier houses generational master karigars (artisans) who practice the sacred arts of Maggam work, Aari needlecraft, Zardozi wirework, and resham thread embroidery.

Whether you bring us a cherished family heirloom saree or a contemporary designer lehenga, we carefully analyze every weave, border, and color undertone to compose a blouse that harmonizes flawlessly.

Our Craftsmanship Ethos

We take pride in preserving traditional techniques while delivering contemporary fit and luxurious comfort.

Authentic Silk & Zari

We work exclusively with high-grade raw silks, pure metallic cords, and durable backing fabrics that preserve their sheen for decades.

Architectural Fit

Our master pattern cutters design structural cups, clean armhole transitions, and customized neck depths for zero gaping.
