Atelier Process

How Customization Works

Every custom piece is handled personally by our head designer. We ensure the embroidery density, motifs, and metal highlights perfectly match your saree drape.

01

Share Your Inspiration

Upload reference photos of your saree body, border, or dream neckline design.

02

Discuss Your Design

03

Master artisans execute intricate needlework with regular progress photos sent to you.

04

Final quality audit, hand-pressed, and dispatched with insured worldwide delivery.
